# Privacy — Plervo PDF Microsoft Store edition

Updated October 20, 2026. This notice describes the standalone Plervo PDF Store edition. Publication and Microsoft certification are pending.

PDF reading, editing and bundled OCR are processed on your computer. Opening a local PDF does not upload it to Plervo. The app does not require a Plervo account or contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app stores preferences and recent-document information locally to restore your workspace. Documents are saved to the location you choose. Do not include personal documents or secrets in public bug reports.

Microsoft Store handles installation and updates. Choosing **Check for updates** opens the Store's updates page. Microsoft's account, download and Store services are governed by [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

When you choose an external link in a document, help or support action, your browser connects to that destination. Optional network-dependent document workflows connect to the services you select; those services have their own privacy practices. PDF editing and bundled OCR do not depend on those services.

**Support** opens [AlporT's Buy Me a Coffee page](https://buymeacoffee.com/alport) only when selected. Support is optional and does not unlock app features. Payments are handled by that service; Plervo does not collect or store payment credentials.

Automation is disabled by default. The app's UI control channel requires explicit launch with `--control`, binds only to loopback and uses a per-session token. The separate CLI MCP interface also requires explicit launch. A local agent you authorize can access the documents and operations you give it permission to use.

For questions, use the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/alportblu/plervo-pdf/issues) without attaching private data. Security concerns can be [reported privately](https://github.com/alportblu/plervo-pdf/security).
